// OVERVIEW

Celeste is a precision platformer about a young woman named Madeline climbing Celeste Mountain. Each screen is a short, demanding challenge built around jumping, climbing walls and an air dash.

The game is difficult, but failure is quick and painless: Madeline respawns instantly at the start of the screen. Alongside the climb, the story explores anxiety and self-doubt.

// HISTORY

Celeste began as a small game made in four days for the PICO-8 fantasy console during a game jam. Maddy Thorson and Noel Berry later expanded the idea into a full game.

The full version was released in 2018. It received wide praise and won Best Independent Game at The Game Awards that year. A free additional chapter, Farewell, was released in 2019.

ARCHIVE NOTE // HISTORICAL IMPACT

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Celeste showed that a demanding game could also be welcoming. Its Assist Mode lets players slow the game down or add extra dashes without judgement.

It is often held up as an example of how mechanics and story can reinforce each other, with the difficulty of the climb reflecting Madeline's personal struggle.
