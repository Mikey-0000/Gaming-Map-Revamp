// OVERVIEW

Doom is a first-person shooter in which a space marine fights through military bases on the moons of Mars after an invasion by demons. Players find weapons and keycards while moving quickly through maze-like levels.

The game is fast and aggressive. Levels mix tight corridors, open arenas and secret areas, and players can also battle each other over a network in a mode called deathmatch.

// HISTORY

Doom was developed by id Software, whose team included programmer John Carmack and designer John Romero. It was released in December 1993, with the first episode distributed as shareware so players could try it for free.

Doom's engine displayed varied heights, lighting and textures at a speed that impressed players at the time. The team designed the game so its levels and graphics could be modified, and players quickly began creating their own content.

ARCHIVE NOTE // HISTORICAL IMPACT

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Doom popularised the first-person shooter so strongly that similar games were often called 'Doom clones' for years afterwards.

Its support for player-made levels and modifications helped create the modding communities that remain important to PC gaming today.
