// OVERVIEW

Street Fighter II is a one-on-one fighting game in which players choose from a cast of fighters from around the world. Each character has a distinct style, normal attacks and special moves performed with specific joystick motions.

Matches are best-of-three rounds. Winning depends on knowing your character, spacing attacks well and predicting what your opponent will do next.

// HISTORY

Street Fighter II was developed by Capcom and released in arcades in 1991, following the original Street Fighter from 1987. The first version offered eight playable fighters, each with their own moves and stage.

The game was enormously popular in arcades and on home consoles, and Capcom released several updated versions with new characters and balance changes. Players also discovered that some attacks could be linked together into combos, which became a core part of the genre.

ARCHIVE NOTE // HISTORICAL IMPACT

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Street Fighter II established the template for the competitive fighting game: a varied cast, special moves and head-to-head play.

It created a lasting competitive community in arcades, which later grew into today's fighting game tournaments.
