// OVERVIEW

RollerCoaster Tycoon is a management game in which the player builds and runs a theme park. Players place rides, shops, paths and scenery, set prices and hire staff to keep everything working.

Its most famous feature is the coaster designer. Players build tracks piece by piece, and guests react to the result, so a coaster that is too intense can make them feel sick.

// HISTORY

RollerCoaster Tycoon was created by Chris Sawyer, who had previously made Transport Tycoon. He wrote almost all of the game himself in assembly language, which allowed it to simulate large numbers of guests on modest computers.

The game was released for Windows in 1999 and became one of the best-selling PC games of its period, followed by expansions and sequels.

ARCHIVE NOTE // HISTORICAL IMPACT

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

RollerCoaster Tycoon is one of the defining management simulations, combining a creative sandbox with readable feedback from thousands of individual guests.

It is also remembered as a remarkable technical achievement: a large commercial game written almost entirely by one programmer in assembly language.
