// OVERVIEW

SimCity is a simulation game in which the player acts as the mayor of a growing city. Instead of placing every building, the player zones land as residential, commercial or industrial and provides roads, power and services.

The city's residents respond to those decisions. Traffic, pollution, crime and taxes all affect where people choose to live and work, and occasional disasters test how well the city was planned.

// HISTORY

SimCity was designed by Will Wright. The idea came from building levels for his earlier game Raid on Bungeling Bay, when he found that he enjoyed creating the cities more than playing the game itself.

Publishers were initially unsure about a game with no clear way to win. Wright co-founded Maxis to release it, and SimCity launched for the Amiga and Macintosh in 1989 before reaching many other platforms.

ARCHIVE NOTE // HISTORICAL IMPACT

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

SimCity popularised the city-building genre and showed that a game could be open-ended, with goals set by the player rather than the designer.

Its approach to simulation led to an entire family of 'Sim' games and influenced management and strategy games for decades.
