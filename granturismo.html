// OVERVIEW

Gran Turismo is a racing game focused on realistic driving. Players earn money by winning races, buy and tune cars, and work their way up through increasingly demanding championships.

Before entering many events, players must pass licence tests that teach braking, cornering and racing lines. The game featured well over a hundred licensed cars from real manufacturers.

// HISTORY

Gran Turismo was developed by Polyphony Digital, led by Kazunori Yamauchi, and released for the PlayStation in Japan in 1997. The team spent several years tuning how each car handled.

The game became the best-selling game on the original PlayStation and started a long-running series that continues today.

ARCHIVE NOTE // HISTORICAL IMPACT

// HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Gran Turismo brought realistic handling and car collecting to a mass audience on a home console.

It set expectations for detail and authenticity in racing games and helped establish the PlayStation as a home for ambitious, high-production games.
